Designed and built a functional arithmetic logic unit (ALU), a core processor component, combining digital logic theory with hands-on prototyping.

## What I did

- **Circuit design:** built a 4-bit ALU from NAND, NOR, and XOR logic capable of addition, subtraction, AND, OR, and bitwise negation, using quad NAND ICs, LEDs, and resistors.
- **Schematics and debugging:** drew full circuit diagrams and iterated to fix carry propagation, signal interference, and power efficiency issues.
- **Prototyping:** wired the ALU by hand on breadboards, with LEDs giving real-time feedback on inputs, the selected operation, and the output.
