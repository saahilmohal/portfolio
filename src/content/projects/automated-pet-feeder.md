## Highlights

- Designed and fabricated a custom 2-layer PCB featuring dual buck converters to regulate 20 V input. Integrated logic-level N-channel MOSFETs for high-current switching, with flyback diodes and decoupling capacitors to isolate sensitive logic from inductive motor noise.
